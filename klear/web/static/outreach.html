Where outreach could help most

Pick a program, then House or Senate. KLEAR puts the districts into groups by how many people meet the program’s income test. In a group, the ranges overlap. So the data cannot say which of those districts has more.

How to read the groups

  • Each figure is a survey estimate. The ± shows its 90% margin of error.
  • The top group starts with the highest estimate. It holds every district whose range overlaps that one. The next group starts with the highest district left, and so on.
  • Districts are listed by number inside a group, not by size.
  • A count only shows who meets the income test. Each program has other rules too.
  • Pick a district to see its full profile and ask about it.
