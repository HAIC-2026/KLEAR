KLEAR / Sources

Data and sources

Every figure KLEAR shows comes from one of these public sources. Survey years vary by measure. The Geography column says whether a source publishes figures for each district, KLEAR estimates them for districts, or the source covers only counties.

The source list appears here once the data store is built.

  • Where a Census figure equals the one in DBEDT’s published district, county, or state profile, its source says so and links to that profile.
  • Unemployment from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and state tax credit claims from the Hawaiʻi Department of Taxation are published only for counties. KLEAR labels them county-level and never shows them as a district’s figure.
  • Where a figure comes from ZIP code or census tract data estimated for a district, KLEAR shows a range, not a single number.
  • Modeled health and needs estimates from CDC PLACES are shown as ranges too. How KLEAR estimates these ranges.
  • Missing data stays missing. KLEAR does not fill gaps with guesses.
  • Program links go to each program’s official page. KLEAR does not decide anyone’s eligibility.
  • For Hawaiʻi public data that is not published by district, such as crime, schools, energy or state budgets, try datatrove.ai (external site), a HAIC 2026 sponsor.

Every check, the build date, and the checks still waiting on files are listed in the validation report (external site).

Terms of use

Most of this data comes from U.S. government agencies and is free for anyone to use. Each source’s terms are linked in the KLEAR project notes (external site). This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.
