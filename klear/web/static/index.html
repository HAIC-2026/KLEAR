WHAT IS KLEAR?

Local insight,
grounded in evidence.

KLEAR is a public-data assistant for Hawaiʻi’s legislators, legislative staff, and economists. Ask about a district’s residents, housing, income, and existing support programs. Every figure is tied to a source, year, and its uncertainty.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

Good questions deserve
traceable answers.

KLEAR draws on the U.S. Census and ACS, Hawaiʻi DBEDT, IRS, HUD, CMS, CDC PLACES, HRSA, and published program rules. Data years and district coverage vary by measure.

Where ZIP or tract data is estimated for a district, KLEAR shows a range. Missing data stays missing.
