03 / THE EVIDENCE

Good questions deserve traceable answers.

KLEAR draws on the U.S. Census and ACS, Hawaiʻi DBEDT, IRS, HUD, CMS, CDC PLACES, HRSA, and published program rules. Data years and district coverage vary by measure.

Where ZIP or tract data is estimated for a district, KLEAR shows a range. Missing data stays missing.
